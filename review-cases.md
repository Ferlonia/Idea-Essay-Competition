# Title Review Cases from Mentor Material

Participant identities were removed in the source material. The judge scores in the first section are anecdotal examples from the material and their scale is not explained; use them as illustration, not as a numeric benchmark. Titles stay in the original Indonesian, with English glosses [ADDED].

## Research-style titles

| Title form | Judge comment | Score |
|---|---|---|
| "Efektivitas Penambahan ... terhadap Perubahan pH Tanah dan Pertumbuhan Tanaman ..." (Effectiveness of adding ... on soil pH change and plant growth) | Wrong concept: the essay takes the form of an idea, not research | 10 |
| "Efektivitas Ekstrak ... sebagai Zat Pengatur Tumbuh Alami terhadap Pertumbuhan ..." (Effectiveness of ... extract as a natural growth regulator on the growth of ...) | Do not use effectiveness, efficiency, influence; it suits a thesis | 14 |
| "Pengaruh Filtrat ... terhadap pH Pakan ..." (Influence of ... filtrate on feed pH) | The word "influence" points to research; the conclusion and discussion all become research | 9 |

Lesson: a research-style title makes the whole manuscript be read through a research lens. The mentor says a judge may be reluctant to read a manuscript whose title is research.

## Case A: dairy marketplace app

- Initial: "SusuMoo: Aplikasi Marketplace Produk Susu sebagai Solusi Peningkatan Efisiensi Distribusi dan Konsumsi Susu di Indonesia dalam Upaya Mewujudkan SDGs 3 dan 8"
- Note: do not use "efficiency" too readily; focus on one goal.
- Result: "SusuMoo: Aplikasi Marketplace dalam Upaya Peningkatan Distribusi Produk Susu dan Turunannya untuk Membantu Kesejahteraan Petani Sapi Lokal di Indonesia" (SusuMoo: a marketplace app to improve distribution of milk products and derivatives to help the welfare of local dairy farmers in Indonesia)
- Lesson: one main goal, an end goal with impact, and layered SDGs claims removed from the title.

## Case B: pumpkin and butterfly-pea tea

- Initial: "TeBuLaKu (Teh Bunga Labu Kuning): Substitusi Daun Labu Kuning (Latin name) dengan bunga telang (Latin name) sebagai bahan dasar pembuatan teh yang kaya (?) sumber antioksidan untuk para penderita diabetes" (TeBuLaKu (pumpkin flower tea): substituting pumpkin leaf (Latin name) with butterfly-pea flower (Latin name) as the base for tea that is rich (?) in antioxidant sources for people with diabetes)
- Note: the acronym must not carry a double meaning; "substitution" is replaced; "rich (?)" is vague; the expansion in parentheses and the Latin names are struck out.
- Result: "TEBULAKU: Diversifikasi Teh Tinggi Antioksidan menggunakan Bahan Dasar Labu Kuning dan Bunga Telang sebagai Langkah Preventif untuk Mengurangi Gejala pada Penderita Diabetes" (TEBULAKU: diversifying high-antioxidant tea using pumpkin and butterfly-pea flower as a preventive step to reduce symptoms in people with diabetes)
- Lesson: the acronym is consistent with the content, terms are definite, Latin names move into the body, and the goal is concrete.

## Case C: health-center complaint platform

- Initial: "SPEED (Sistem Pengaduan Efektif dan Efisien berupa Digital): Peningkatan Layanan di Puskesmas ..." (SPEED (effective and efficient digital complaint system): improving services at community health centers ...)
- Note: the expansion is unnecessary; an acronym has three words at most; "Improving services" must bring out the problem; the direction of the title is confusing and needs a goal.
- Result: "SPEED: Platform Pengaduan Kinerja Staff Puskesmas sebagai Upaya Peningkatan Layanan Publik di Kabupaten Banda Aceh" (SPEED: a platform for complaints about health-center staff performance as an effort to improve public services in Banda Aceh Regency)
- Lesson: name the form of the idea (platform), the problem (staff performance), the goal (better public service), and the scope (regency).

## Summary of correction patterns

Remove research/effective/efficient words; one goal; a short acronym with a single meaning; no expansion in parentheses; no Latin names in the title; explicit problem and goal; clear scope; definite terms.
