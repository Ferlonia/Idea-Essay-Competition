# Introduction and Discussion Guide

Contents: introduction (content, composition, 5-paragraph pattern, data), discussion (items, guidance per type of idea, order, writing rules).

## Introduction

### Required content
The problem, urgency, recent data, a case (a year's worth with data if possible), recent references, fit with the theme, fit with the subtheme, novelty of the idea, strength of the idea, focus of the idea, and direction of continuation.

### Composition
About 30% author's opinion and 70% data/facts. Every important claim is supported by cited data.

### Form (mentor suggestion)
1.5-2 pages, ideally 5 paragraphs, 3-5 sentences per paragraph.

### The 5-paragraph pattern **[ADDED]**
- P1: general context and national data/theme (recent facts, citation)
- P2: local data and case on the problem raised
- P3: urgency and consequences if left unhandled; connect with the theme and subtheme
- P4: existing solutions and their gap; introduce the idea and its novelty
- P5: strength of the idea, focus of the idea, direction of continuation, transition to the discussion

The example introduction in the mentor material uses BPS data, a case in the relevant regency, legal/regulatory references, a solution initiative, and a link to the SDGs with a supporting figure.

### Data and references
Use sources from the last 5 years counted from today's date; older sources need a strong reason. Do not invent figures. If data is missing: search official sources (BPS, ministries, regional offices, reputable journals) or mark `[DATA NEEDED: ...]`.

## Discussion

The longest section and the foundation of creativity (the heaviest-weighted aspect according to the mentor; match it to the organizers' rubric if there is one).

### Items that may be included (choose the relevant ones)
Explanation of the idea; overview of the idea; SWOT analysis; implementation; advantages; differences from existing ideas; the SMART method (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time-bound; written "Acc" in the source material); product content; app/website features; prototype/design; supporting parties (stakeholders); competitor analysis; fulfillment of the subtheme; fulfillment of the theme; success projection; innovation and creativity.

### Guidance per type of idea
- Product: composition, materials and their sources, how it works, prototype/design.
- App/website: features, user flow, mockup, managing party.
- Program/method/system: implementation stages, implementers, target, schedule, SMART indicators.

### Suggested order **[ADDED]**
1. Overview and explanation of the idea (what, how it works, uniqueness)
2. Fulfillment of the theme and subtheme
3. Differences and novelty, including competitor analysis
4. Design/prototype/features/content
5. Implementation: steps, supporters, SMART targets
6. SWOT analysis
7. Success and sustainability projection
8. Statement of innovation and creativity

### Writing rules
- State creativity explicitly: what is new, why it is different, the measurable benefit.
- Stay an idea and a design; avoid a research-report style (test results, statistical tables of experiments). Write benefit figures as sourced projections, not experimental results.
- Tables, charts, or design figures are allowed; if they do not fit, move them to the appendix.
- Chapter title: the mentor suggests PEMBAHASAN (Discussion); ISI (Body) is also acceptable. Follow the competition guidelines if they require a specific name.
