## Patterns visible (Claude's analysis)

Almost all of them contain (a) a specific form of idea (pesticide, device, app, program, method), (b) a clear material or technology, (c) a goal in the form "untuk/guna (for) + Mengurangi (Reduce) / Memaksimalkan (Maximize) / Menguatkan (Strengthen) / Mendukung (Support)", (d) a target or scope (farmers, MSMEs, a community, a regency), and (e) an easy-to-remember acronym or brand name. None uses a research-marker word.

## Warning: examples that do not follow their own rules

Several titles in the bank break the rules in titles.md. Do not imitate them on those points, and do not use them to argue against a correction:

- Titles 3, 4, 6, 11, and 14 write the expansion or an explanation of the acronym in parentheses or quotation marks (rule: do not).
- Title 12 contains the Latin name of a bacterium (rule: move it to the body).
- All the titles fall between 15 and 24 words (within the 25 ceiling), but titles 7 and 12 are very technical; consider the readers before imitating them.
- Titles 4 and 5 contain the word "untuk" (for) twice, which blurs the goal.

The likely explanation: these examples were collected as "potential winners" and are not the result of mentor corrections. If the user points to these examples as justification, explain that the mentor rules in the review material are more specific.
