# Title Guide for Idea Essays

Contents: mandatory rules, research-marker words, five components, three styles, acronym rules, validation procedure, output format, additional notes, worked example.

## 1. Mandatory rules

- Follow the structure and styles below; do not invent freely, because judges are used to reading this pattern.
- The title must not look like research (see the word list in section 2).
- Target 20 words, ceiling 25. More than 25 words is judged less good.
- It must carry a problem that matches the chosen SUBTHEME and stay inside the THEME.

## 2. Research-marker words (avoid in the title)

Pengaruh (influence/effect), Efektivitas/Efektifitas (effectiveness), Efisiensi (efficiency), Dampak (impact), Analisis (analysis), Hubungan (relationship), Evaluasi (evaluation), Studi (study), Fenomena (phenomenon), Korelasi (correlation), Regresi (regression), ANOVA, Rancangan Acak (randomized design), DMRT.

[ADDED] For an English-language title the same words apply: "effect of", "influence of", "effectiveness of", "efficiency of", "impact of", "analysis of", "relationship between", "evaluation of", "study of", "correlation", "regression".

Mentor note: do not use "efficiency" or "effective" as the name of the goal too easily. Replace them with a concrete goal, for example "improving distribution" or "reducing pesticide use". The word "substitusi" (substitution) was also once asked to be replaced (for example with "diversifikasi", diversification).

## 3. Five components

1. Name of the idea/innovation (may be an acronym)
2. Explanation of the idea (its form and what it is based on or made of)
3. The problem raised
4. The goal of the idea (one main goal)
5. Scope (optional): location, target group, level of application

Worked example: "KOWLIA TRAP: Teknologi Perangkap Hama dari Kombinasi Yellow Sticky Trap dan Light Trap Berbasis IoT sebagai Pengendali Hama untuk Mengurangi Penggunaan Pestisida di Tingkat Petani" (KOWLIA TRAP: pest-trap technology combining a yellow sticky trap and an IoT-based light trap as pest control to reduce pesticide use at the farmer level)
- Name: KOWLIA TRAP
- Explanation: pest-trap technology combining a yellow sticky trap and an IoT-based light trap
- Problem: pests
- Goal: reduce pesticide use
- Scope: at the farmer level

## 4. Three styles (formulas)

Parentheses mean the connecting word is optional. The Indonesian connectors (sebagai = as, untuk/guna = for) are kept because the title is normally written in Indonesian.

- **Style 1:** Acronym/Idea: Optimalisasi/Pemanfaatan (Optimization/Utilization of) + Potential + (sebagai) + Product/Item + (untuk) + Problem + goal
- **Style 2:** Optimalisasi/Pemanfaatan + Potential + (sebagai) + "Acronym/Idea" + (untuk/guna) + Problem + goal
- **Style 3:** Optimalisasi/Pemanfaatan + Potential + (sebagai) + Product/Idea + (untuk) + Problem + goal

Practical difference: Style 1 opens with the idea's name followed by a colon; Style 2 puts the idea's name in quotation marks in the middle; Style 3 has no acronym. The opening word does not have to be "Optimalisasi/Pemanfaatan": "Inovasi" (Innovation), "Program", "Teknik" (Technique), "Aplikasi" (Application), "Metode" (Method), and "Peningkatan Nilai Tambah" (Increasing Added Value) also appear in good examples, as long as they are not research-marker words.

## 5. Acronym rules

- Allowed, but with no double meaning and consistent with the content of the idea.
- Do not write the expansion in parentheses in the title (it wastes words).
- The acronym's base is three words at most; easy to say and remember.

## 6. Validation procedure (run on every title)

1. Count the words (target 20, ceiling 25).
2. Check for research-marker words.
3. Check the components: is there a form of the idea, a problem, and a goal? (scope optional)
4. Check there is only one main goal (not "distribution and consumption" together; do not pile up SDGs claims).
5. Check the fit with the theme and subtheme.
6. Check the acronym (double meaning? long? expansion in parentheses?).
7. Check ambiguous terms or question marks; replace them with definite terms (for example "rich in (?)" becomes "high in antioxidants").
8. Check for Latin names and excessive technical detail; move them into the body of the manuscript.

## 7. Output format

A table with the columns: Check | Result (Pass/Fix) | Note. Under it, three alternative titles (Style 1, 2, 3), each with its word count. Count words carefully (split on spaces); if in doubt, count with a simple tool.

## 8. Additional notes: word limit and title types [ADDED]

* A mentor annotation on one example essay says the title should be 20 words at most, while the title material says target 20 and ceiling 25. Use 20 as the safe target; the hard limit follows the competition guidelines. If the guidelines are silent, 25 is the upper limit.
* The title types judges tend to like, and the lessons from example titles that break the rules, are in intro-closing-breakdown.md (sections "Title types judges tend to like" and "Critical notes").
* [ADDED] Word counts differ between languages. Count in the language the title will be submitted in, and use the competition's own counting rule if it gives one.

## 9. Worked validation example [ADDED]

Input title (from the review case in review-cases.md, Case A): "SusuMoo: Aplikasi Marketplace Produk Susu sebagai Solusi Peningkatan Efisiensi Distribusi dan Konsumsi Susu di Indonesia dalam Upaya Mewujudkan SDGs 3 dan 8" (SusuMoo: a dairy-product marketplace app as a solution for improving the efficiency of milk distribution and consumption in Indonesia, in an effort to achieve SDGs 3 and 8)

| Check | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Word count | Pass | 22 words: within the 25 ceiling, above the 20 target |
| Research-marker words | Fix | "Efisiensi" (efficiency); replace with a concrete goal |
| Components | Fix | Idea form and name are clear; the problem is vague and the goal is blurred |
| One main goal | Fix | Two goals (distribution and consumption) plus two SDGs; keep one |
| Theme and subtheme | Pass | Confirm against the actual subtheme |
| Acronym | Pass | "SusuMoo" is short; one reading |
| Ambiguous terms | Pass | None |
| Latin names, technical detail | Pass | None |

Corrected title (the mentor's result): "SusuMoo: Aplikasi Marketplace dalam Upaya Peningkatan Distribusi Produk Susu dan Turunannya untuk Membantu Kesejahteraan Petani Sapi Lokal di Indonesia" (19 words) (SusuMoo: a marketplace app to improve distribution of milk products and derivatives to help the welfare of local dairy farmers in Indonesia).
